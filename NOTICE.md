# Notices and credits

## Upstream code

This repository contains documentation, a theme, screenshots, and small patches/copies of files from
[Gathin23/cleanyourtv](https://github.com/Gathin23/cleanyourtv), which is licensed under the MIT License.

The upstream project is the source of the launcher, the debloat/recovery tools, the theme format, and
the Weather add-on. This repository does not redistribute their full source tree or their APKs.

The Weather add-on was originally authored by
[Sumyabazar1](https://github.com/Sumyabazar1) and is part of the same upstream project.

## Wallpaper

`theme/nebula-coast/wallpaper.jpg` is an adaptation of:

- **“Manta in German Channel Palau”** by **amanderson2**
- Source: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Manta_in_German_Channel_Palau_(12672994784).jpg>
- License: **CC BY 2.0** — <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/>

The image was cropped to 16:9 and resized for a 1920×1080 display. The launcher applies a dark overlay
(`wallpaper_dim`) so the clock, weather, and dock remain readable.

## Screenshot redactions

The before/after screenshots in `docs/assets/` are sanitized:

- The profile avatar in the original launcher screenshot was replaced with a neutral placeholder.
- The city label in the weather screenshot was replaced with `YOUR CITY`.
- No account names, email addresses, IP addresses, device serial numbers, VPN profiles, or credentials
  are present.
